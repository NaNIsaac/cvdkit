# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- `simulate` with the Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009) matrices for protan, deutan and tritan
  deficiencies at any severity, for figures, images and colours.
- `separation`, `distinguishable` and `check`: lines and markers must differ in lightness or hue
  under every simulation, filled areas in CIEDE2000.
- `pick`, `style_cycler` and `hatch_cycler`: colours with markers, dash patterns and hatches.
- Palettes from Okabe and Ito, Tol and Petroff; Tol's diverging colormaps.
